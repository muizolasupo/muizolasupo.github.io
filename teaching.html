My approach

I use guided worksheets and applied examples to help students understand the reasoning behind an answer. The aim is to build confidence with economic intuition, graphs, and formal analysis.

Price controls and shortages

Using a price ceiling to show how a gap opens between quantity demanded and quantity supplied, and why the shortage persists while the ceiling binds.

Supply and demand in a pricing decision

An applied problem for a full lecture hall: students use market research on subscriptions to judge what price a new app can sustain.

Setting up the firm's problem

Framing a firm's production choices in plain language before moving to the formal model.

From production to cost curves

Deriving a competitive firm's cost curves from a Cobb-Douglas production function and connecting them to the market price.
