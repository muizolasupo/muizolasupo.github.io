My approach

I use guided worksheets and applied examples to help students understand the reasoning behind an answer. The aim is to build confidence with economic intuition, graphs, and formal analysis.

Average and marginal product

Deriving average and marginal product from y = 10L² − L³, and showing that marginal product exceeds average product until L = 5, where average product peaks.
